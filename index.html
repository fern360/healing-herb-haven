How we source

We buy direct from family farms in Oregon, Bulgaria, Egypt and India, and test every lot for identity and purity before it reaches our shelves.

How we pack

Herbs are weighed to order and sealed in resealable, light-blocking kraft pouches to keep their color, aroma and potency.

How to store

Keep herbs cool, dark and dry. Leaves and flowers are best within a year; roots and powders keep up to two.
